---
title: "Create a Commission Rule"
description: "Override the global commission for a specific store, product type, or category."
---

A commission rule overrides the global commission for a specific scope, such as a single store or a product category. When several rules could apply to a sale, the most specific rule wins. If no rule matches, the global commission is used. This guide creates a rule from the Admin Panel.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the commissions settings">
    Go to **Settings**, then **Commissions** in the sidebar. The page shows your global commission and any rules you have created. Select **Create** in the **Commission Rules** section to start a new one.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/user-guide/admin/create-a-commission-rule/step-1.png" alt="Open the commissions settings" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the create form">
    The rule form opens with two steps: **Details** defines what the rule applies to, and **Commission** sets the fee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the rule details">
    In **Details**, enter a **Title** you will recognise later and a unique **Code**. Then open **Type** to choose the scope: a store, a product type, a category, or a store combined with one of those. Depending on the scope, select the specific stores, product types, or categories the rule applies to.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/user-guide/admin/create-a-commission-rule/step-3.png" alt="Enter the rule details" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the fee">
    Select **Continue**. In **Commission**, choose **Percentage** to charge a share of each order total, or **Fixed** to charge a set amount per order. Enter the **Value**: a number between 0 and 100 for a percentage, or an amount per store currency for a fixed fee. Set **Tax included** and **Shipping included** as needed, the same way as the global commission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the rule">
    Select **Save**. The new rule appears in the **Commission Rules** list and applies immediately to matching sales.
  </Step>
</Steps>

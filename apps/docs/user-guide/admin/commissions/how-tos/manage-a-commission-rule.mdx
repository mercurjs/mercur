---
title: "Manage a Commission Rule"
description: "Edit, enable, disable, or delete an existing commission rule."
---

Commission rules are managed from the Commissions settings page. Each rule overrides the global commission for its scope, and the most specific matching rule always wins.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the commissions settings">
    Go to **Settings**, then **Commissions**. Your rules appear in the **Commission Rules** section below the global commission.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/user-guide/admin/manage-a-commission-rule/step-1.png" alt="Open the commissions settings" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit, enable, disable, or delete">
    Open a rule from the **Commission Rules** list to reach its detail page, or use the row's actions menu. From there you can **Edit** the rule's scope and fee, **Enable** or **Disable** it, or **Delete** it. A disabled rule stops applying without being removed, so you can turn it back on later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

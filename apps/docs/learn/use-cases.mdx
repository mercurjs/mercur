Mercur fits two kinds of business. Pick the one that matches yours; the modules underneath are the same.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Marketplace platform">
    You are building a marketplace from scratch, or replacing one. Mercur runs everything: the storefront, the operator admin, the vendor panel sellers log into, and the backend that splits every checkout into one order per seller, computes commissions and pays sellers out.

    <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/figures/use-case-platform-light.svg" alt="Three Mercur surfaces stacked back to front: the admin Sellers list, the vendor panel's Offers table for ACME, and the storefront product page for the Linen shirt listing the offers a shopper can buy." />
    <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/figures/use-case-platform-dark.svg" alt="Three Mercur surfaces stacked back to front: the admin Sellers list, the vendor panel's Offers table for ACME, and the storefront product page for the Linen shirt listing the offers a shopper can buy." />

    **Pick this when**

    - You don't have a store yet, or you want the marketplace to be the product
    - Sellers should get their own panel: offers, orders, fulfillment, payouts
    - You want one shared catalog that every seller lists against

    **What you get**

    | Surface | Who uses it | Package |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Storefront | Shoppers | Any framework on the Store API, or the starter storefront |
    | Admin panel | Operators | `@mercurjs/admin` |
    | Vendor panel | Sellers | `@mercurjs/vendor` |
    | Backend | Everyone | `@mercurjs/core` on Medusa |

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Marketplace inside your store">
    You already sell your own products on a platform like Magento. You want third-party sellers next to them, in the same product page and the same checkout, without moving your catalog or your payments. Your store stays the storefront; Mercur becomes the marketplace layer behind it.

    <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/figures/use-case-hybrid-light.svg" alt="A product page in your own store lists your warehouse next to two seller offers from ACME and GLOBEX. A wire leads from the seller rows to a Mercur order group with one order per seller; your own line never leaves your store." />
    <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/figures/use-case-hybrid-dark.svg" alt="A product page in your own store lists your warehouse next to two seller offers from ACME and GLOBEX. A wire leads from the seller rows to a Mercur order group with one order per seller; your own line never leaves your store." />

    **Pick this when**

    - Your store, checkout and payment provider are already live and should stay
    - You sell first-party stock and want sellers to compete on the same SKUs
    - Sellers need a panel, but shoppers should never notice a second system

    **Who runs what**

    | Concern | Your store | Mercur |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Catalog, prices, stock of your own products | ✔ | |
    | Storefront, cart, checkout, payment capture | ✔ | |
    | Sellers, their offers, their panel | | ✔ |
    | One order per seller, commissions, payouts | | ✔ |

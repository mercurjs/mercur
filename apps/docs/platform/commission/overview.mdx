Use Mercur to decide what share of each sale the marketplace keeps and what each seller earns.

Commission is the marketplace’s cut of a seller order. You define rates as a percentage or a fixed amount and scope them with rules to sellers, products, types, collections, or categories. When an order is placed, Mercur picks the most specific matching rate for every line item and writes a commission line with the amount. The payout for that order is the order total minus the sum of its commission lines.

<Note>
  The Commission module owns four data models: `CommissionRate` (`comrate`), `CommissionRule` (`comrule`), `CommissionRateValue` (`comval`), and `CommissionLine` (`comline`). Resolve its service with `MercurModules.COMMISSION` from `@mercurjs/types`.
</Note>

## Key features

The Commission module covers configuration, resolution, and the per-order record:

- **Percentage or fixed rates**: a percent of the line subtotal, or a flat amount per line
- **Per-currency amounts**: a fixed rate holds one amount per currency and falls back to its `value`
- **Five scoping dimensions**: rules match on `product`, `product_type`, `product_collection`, `product_category`, and `seller`
- **Most specific wins**: the matching rate scoped on the most dimensions wins, and ties go to the oldest rate
- **Global commission**: a seeded `is_default` rate that can’t be deleted and is the only rate that commissions shipping
- **Lines that follow the order**: lines are written at checkout and recomputed on order edits, returns, claims, and exchanges
- **Exact arithmetic**: amounts are computed with Medusa’s `MathBN` and stored as big numbers
- **Pluggable calculation**: swap the built-in `system` provider for your own `ICommissionProvider`

## How it works

Commission runs as part of checkout and again whenever the order changes:

1. `completeCartWithSplitOrdersWorkflow` splits the cart into one order per seller.
2. It runs `refreshOrderCommissionLinesWorkflow` for the new orders as a nested step.
3. The workflow builds one calculation context per order and passes them to the active provider in one batch.
4. The `system` provider loads the enabled rates once and picks the winning rate for each item.
5. The module replaces the order’s commission lines with the new set.
6. When a payout is created, Mercur subtracts the sum of the order’s commission lines from the order total.

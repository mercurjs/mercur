Use Mercur to connect each seller to a payment provider and send them their share of every order.

A payout is a transfer from the marketplace to one seller, for one order. The Payout module holds 3 records: the seller’s payout account, its onboarding state, and the payouts made to it. Every external call goes through a single payout provider. Stripe Connect ships as a package, and a built-in `system` provider runs when you register none.

<Note>
  Resolve the module service with `MercurModules.PAYOUT` from `@mercurjs/types`. Import the payout workflows from `@mercurjs/core/workflows`.
</Note>

## Key features

The Payout module covers a seller’s connection to the provider and each transfer made through it:

- **One provider interface**: `IPayoutProvider` has 4 methods, and the module loads exactly one implementation
- **Stripe Connect provider**: `@mercurjs/stripe-connector-plugin` creates Express accounts, onboarding and login links, and transfers
- **One account per store**: `createPayoutAccountWorkflow` refuses a second account for the same seller
- **Re-runnable onboarding**: `createOnboardingWorkflow` creates the onboarding record once and updates it after that
- **Webhook-driven statuses**: provider webhooks move accounts and payouts between statuses through `processPayoutForWebhookWorkflow`
- **Commission-aware amounts**: `createPayoutWorkflow` pays the order total minus the order’s commission lines

## How it works

A seller moves from no account to a paid order in 5 steps:

1. Run `createPayoutAccountWorkflow` for the seller. The provider creates a connected account.
2. Run `createOnboardingWorkflow`. The provider returns onboarding data, such as a Stripe onboarding link.
3. The provider posts webhooks to `/hooks/payout`, and the `payout-webhook` subscriber sets the account to `active`, `restricted`, or `rejected`.
4. Run `createPayoutWorkflow` with an order id. It computes the seller’s share and asks the provider to transfer it.
5. Later provider webhooks update the payout’s status.

<Warning>
  Mercur doesn’t trigger step 4 for you. No scheduled job or subscriber in `@mercurjs/core` captures payments or runs `createPayoutWorkflow`. Call it from your own job, subscriber, or route. See [The payout pipeline](/platform/payout/concepts/payout-pipeline).
</Warning>

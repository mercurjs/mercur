Use return requests to run marketplace returns the way a multi-vendor business
needs them: the customer asks, the seller who shipped the goods decides, and the
marketplace operator issues the refund.

Medusa already models the physical return. A `Return` tracks requested and
received items, return shipping, inventory and the order's balance. What a
marketplace adds on top is the approval layer: who may raise a request, who has to
accept it, and who finalises the money. The Return Request domain is that layer.
Every request wraps a Medusa return, and every transition runs a Medusa workflow,
so inventory, order summaries and payment collections stay stock.

<Note>
  **Return request ≠ return.** The `ReturnRequest` row holds the marketplace
  decision trail. The Medusa `Return` it points to holds the items, shipping and
  received quantities. Read items and shipping from the return, read who decided
  what from the request.
</Note>

## Key features

- **Three initiators:** customers from their order history, sellers on behalf of a customer, and operators on behalf of either.
- **Seller review:** a seller accepts with or without requiring the item back, or declines with a reason.
- **Receive through the standard flow:** items come back through the existing vendor returns endpoints and restock per offer.
- **Operator refund:** the operator approves the request, which refunds the order's captured payment and closes it.
- **Withdrawal:** customers can withdraw while the request is still pending.
- **Feature flags:** `return_request` gates the customer flow and the stock `/store/returns` endpoint, `seller_initiated_return_requests` gates seller-raised requests.

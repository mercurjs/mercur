Use offers to let several stores sell the same catalog product, each on its own terms.

Products in Mercur live in one shared master catalog that no store owns. An offer is the record that connects a store to one master variant. It carries everything that makes the listing the store’s own: its SKU, price, inventory, condition, and shipping profile. The Offer module holds the offer and its conditions, and links reach out to the store, the catalog, pricing, inventory, fulfillment, and the cart and order line items that buy it.

<Note>
  The data model is `Offer` (id prefix `offer`), resolved with `MercurModules.OFFER` from `@mercurjs/types`. A store never owns a product. Two stores selling the same variant each hold their own offer.
</Note>

## Key features

The Offer module covers everything a store needs to sell a catalog variant:

- **Per-store listings**: an offer points at one master variant, with a SKU that’s unique within its store
- **Offer-scoped pricing**: price rows sit on the variant’s shared price set, each tagged with an `offer_id` price rule
- **Offer-scoped inventory**: inventory items link to the offer, not the variant, so stock never mixes between stores
- **Conditions**: operators define conditions such as `new` or `refurbished`, and stores list offers under the active ones
- **Per-offer shipping**: each offer names the store’s own shipping profile
- **Order attribution**: cart and order line items link to the offer that was bought
- **Hooks and events**: every workflow exposes hooks and emits `offer.*` events for your own side effects

## How it works

An offer moves through the marketplace in four steps:

1. A store runs `createOffersWorkflow` against a master variant, with a SKU, prices, inventory items, and a shipping profile.
2. The workflow writes the prices onto the variant’s price set with an `offer_id` rule and creates inventory items linked to the offer.
3. When a shopper adds the offer to a cart, the line item links to the offer and pricing resolves the offer’s own rows.
4. At checkout the order line item keeps the offer link, so the right store fulfills the item and gets paid.

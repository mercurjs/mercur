The Stripe connector plugin runs a marketplace's money on Stripe. The customer pays once for a cart that spans several sellers. Each seller gets their share of every order they fulfill, minus your commission, in a Stripe Connect account. Their earnings reach their bank on a schedule you control.

You install one package, `@mercurjs/stripe-connector-plugin`, in your Mercur API. It registers a payment provider for checkout, a payout provider for seller accounts, the scheduled jobs that decide when money moves, and the admin and vendor screens to watch it.

## Who owns what

| | Stripe | Mercur core | Stripe connector plugin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Card payment, authorization, capture | ✓ | | Decides when to capture |
| Connected accounts, KYC, onboarding forms | ✓ | Mirrors the account as a `payout_account` | Creates accounts and links |
| Seller's share of each order (`payout`) | Moves it as a Transfer | Computes total − commission, keeps the status | Decides when to run |
| Refunds taken back from the seller (`payout_reversal`) | Moves it as a Transfer reversal | Computes the refunded share | Decides when to run |
| Bank payout to the seller | Moves it as a Payout | | Nets each period into a settlement |
| Fees and credits to a seller | Moves them as Transfers | | Adjustments entered by the operator |
| Alerts, job history, retries | | | ✓ |

The platform account is the **Merchant of Record**. Customers see your statement descriptor, and refunds and disputes land on your account first.

## Two layers of money

Every seller sees money move twice, and the plugin keeps both visible:

1. **Per order.** When an order is fulfilled and its payment captured, the seller's share moves from your platform balance to the seller's Stripe balance. A refund later takes the seller's share of it back.
2. **Per period.** Every night the plugin nets everything that reached the seller's balance since the last run (payouts, minus reversals, plus or minus adjustments) into one **settlement**, and sends that amount to the seller's bank.

The per-order layer lets sellers and operators trace every order to the cent. The per-period layer gives the seller one bank deposit per period, with a statement that explains it.

## What you get

- **One payment, split per seller**: the cart is authorized once with manual capture, and captured only when every seller order in it is decided, for the authorized amount minus canceled orders
- **Automatic seller onboarding**: every new seller gets a Stripe Express account and an onboarding link, refreshed when it expires, and a login link once onboarding is done
- **Payouts on fulfillment**: each fulfilled, captured order pays the seller its total minus commission, net of refunds that landed before it
- **Refunds taken back**: each later refund reverses the seller's share of it on the original transfer, net of the commission share
- **Nightly settlements**: one bank payout per seller and currency, held and retried while the balance is short or the account can't receive payouts
- **Adjustments**: subscription fees, extra invoices and extra credits you enter in the admin, sent on the next settlement run
- **Operations screens**: settlements, adjustments, captures and every job's run history under **Integrations → Stripe**, with **Run now** and retry actions
- **Alerts**: a daily email of everything still failed, and KYC monitoring on request
- **A vendor Stripe page**: account setup, what Stripe still needs from the seller, and their settlements

## Compatibility

| Requirement | Version |
| --- | --- |
| Mercur | 2.3.9 and later, with `@mercurjs/stripe-connector-plugin` |
| Node.js | 22.22 and later |
| Stripe | An account with Connect enabled, using Express connected accounts |

## Not supported yet

- Standard and Custom connected accounts. The provider creates Express accounts only
- Separate charges per seller (direct charges or destination charges). The plugin uses one platform charge and separate transfers
- Billing-cycle calendars for settlements. The settlement job runs nightly, and each settlement covers the time since the previous one
- More than one payout provider at a time. The payout module takes exactly one

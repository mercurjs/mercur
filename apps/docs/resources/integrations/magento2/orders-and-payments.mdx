Magento collects the money, and Mercur keeps track of whose it is. The shopper pays once through your Magento payment method. Mercur records that payment on the `pp_external_external` provider, splits it across one order per seller and computes commission. No money moves through Mercur at checkout, so Mercur only follows what Magento reports: an authorization when the order is sent, a capture when it's invoiced, a refund when it's credited.

## The external payment

Each Magento order with seller lines has one payment in Mercur, held by the order group's cart. Its status follows the Magento order:

| Magento | Mercur payment | Route Magento calls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order committed in a configured status | Authorized | `POST /store/carts/:id/complete` |
| Invoice paid | Captured, then split per seller order | `POST /admin/magento/order-groups/:id/payments/capture` |
| Order canceled before capture | Canceled, every seller order canceled | `POST /admin/magento/order-groups/:id/payments/cancel` |
| Credit memo on seller lines | Refund recorded on the seller order | `POST /admin/magento/orders/:id/refunds/external` |

The Magento order number is the payment's reference, and Magento's payment method is stored with it.

## Send the order to Mercur

The workflow starts when Magento commits an order that has seller lines.

1. Magento checks the order's status against **Create in Mercur when the order status is**, `pending` and `processing` by default. Remove `pending` to wait until the payment is confirmed.
2. It adds an authorized payment session on `pp_external_external` to the Mercur cart it built during checkout, and completes the cart.
3. Mercur splits the cart into an order group with one order per seller and computes their commission lines.
4. Magento stores the order group on the order and each seller order on its lines, sets every seller line to `not_fulfilled`, and adds the comment `Marketplace order group {number} created in Mercur.`

Your own lines are never sent. A cart's channel, `magento` by default, is copied onto the order group and every seller order as `metadata.channel`.

If Mercur doesn't answer, the Magento order still succeeds. The comment "Marketplace order could not be created in Mercur: …" explains why, and the `order_retry` process sends it on its next run, every 10 minutes by default. **Send to marketplace** on the order view sends it at once.

## Capture the payment

The workflow starts when an invoice on the order is paid.

1. Magento calls the capture route for the order group.
2. Mercur captures the external payment once and splits it across the seller orders. Calling it again is a no-op.
3. Magento adds the comment "Marketplace payment captured in Mercur."

Capture confirms the whole order group on the first paid invoice. That invoice also carries all the seller shipping that isn't invoiced yet.

A capture that fails is retried by `order_retry`, and the comment says so. When your payment method captures at placement, the invoice is paid before the order reaches Mercur. `order_retry` then captures right after it's sent.

Mercur splits the payment across seller orders only once it's captured, so what each seller is owed follows money you actually collected. See [the payout pipeline](/platform/payout/concepts/payout-pipeline) for how transfers to sellers are triggered.

## Cancel before capture

The workflow starts when you cancel a Magento order that hasn't been captured.

1. Magento calls the cancel route.
2. Mercur cancels every seller order in the group and voids the authorization.
3. Magento adds the comment "Marketplace payment authorization released in Mercur."

After capture the payment can't be canceled. Refund the order with a credit memo instead.

## Fulfillment

Sellers ship their own lines from the vendor panel. Magento records what they report.

The workflow starts when a seller creates, ships, delivers or cancels a fulfillment.

1. Mercur sends `order.fulfillment_created`, `shipment.created`, `delivery.created` or `order.fulfillment_canceled`.
2. Magento reads the seller order with its fulfillments and tracking labels.
3. For each fulfillment that has shipped and isn't recorded yet, it creates a Magento shipment of those lines, with the tracking numbers and a customer-visible comment linking to the tracking pages. The customer is notified the usual way.
4. Each seller line's marketplace status is updated from its quantities: `fulfilled`, `partially_shipped`, `shipped` or `delivered`.

Partial and multi-parcel deliveries become one Magento shipment each, as they happen. The order stays in **Processing** until every fulfillment is recorded.

### Seller lines are shipped by sellers

With **Leave seller lines to the sellers** on, the default, seller lines can't be shipped from the Magento admin or the REST ship endpoint. Your own lines ship as usual. The lock covers shipping only: invoices, cancellations and credit memos work on seller lines as before.

### Resync by hand

**Resync marketplace status** on the order view reads every seller order again and records anything missing. Use it after a webhook outage that lasted longer than Mercur's retries.

## When a seller cancels or refuses

The workflow starts when a seller order is canceled in Mercur.

1. Mercur sends `order.canceled`.
2. Magento sets the seller's lines to `refused` when the seller canceled, or `canceled` when the operator did, and adds an order comment.
3. If those lines were invoiced, Magento issues a credit memo for them and that seller's shipping, refunding the shopper.

## Returns

Return events, `order.return_requested` and `order.return_received`, update the lines to **Return requested** and **Returned** and add an order comment. They don't move money. The refund comes separately, when the seller or operator refunds the order in Mercur.

## Refunds

Refunds can start on either side. Each is executed where the money is, and recorded on the other side, so commission and the seller's balance stay right.

### Refund from Magento

The workflow starts when you create a credit memo that includes seller lines or seller shipping.

1. For each seller order on the credit memo, Magento adds up the refunded seller lines, including tax and minus discounts.
2. It splits the refunded seller shipping across sellers by what each one charged.
3. It records each amount on the seller order through the external refund route, with the credit memo number as the reference.
4. Mercur records the refund on the captured payment, tagged `metadata.source: "external"`, and emits `order.refund_recorded`.

<Warning>
  A report that fails is logged to `var/log/mercur.log` as `Mercur refund report failed` and isn't retried. Record the missing amount on the seller order with `POST /admin/magento/orders/:id/refunds/external`, or the seller's balance will include money you already refunded.
</Warning>

### Refund from Mercur

The workflow starts when a seller or an operator refunds a seller order in Mercur.

1. Mercur records the refund. It has moved no money, so the plugin emits `order.refund_requested` with the order, amount and refund id.
2. Magento issues an adjustment credit memo for the amount on the Magento order. It refunds online through your payment method when the invoice was captured online, and offline otherwise.
3. The credit memo stores the Mercur refund id, so a repeated webhook doesn't refund twice, and it isn't reported back.

When a credit memo isn't possible, the order gets the comment `Mercur refund {id} of {amount} could not be refunded automatically. Refund it manually.`

If the webhook never arrives, the `refunds` process finds the refund within 15 minutes. It checks seller orders on Magento orders updated in the last 30 days that aren't closed or canceled.

## In the Magento admin

Every order with seller lines has a **Marketplace** section: the order group, and per seller the seller order, items, seller shipping, status and tracking. Each item has a marketplace status column, and the order grid has a **Marketplace order group** column.

Three buttons on the order view cover anything that needs a push by hand:

| Button | Shown when | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Send to marketplace | The order isn't in Mercur yet | Sends it now, whatever its status |
| Capture marketplace payment | The order is paid and not yet captured in Mercur | Confirms the capture |
| Resync marketplace status | The order is in Mercur | Reads every seller order again and records missing shipments and statuses |

The buttons need the **Mercur → Order actions** ACL resource.
